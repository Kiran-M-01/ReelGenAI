Every morning, the people of a small village crossed an old wooden bridge to reach the nearby town. For more than a hundred years, the bridge had stood strong against time, and no one ever questioned its safety. But one evening, as dark storm clouds rolled in and rain began to fall, a young traveler became the last person to cross. Halfway across the bridge, he heard a loud crack behind him. A wooden plank split apart and disappeared into the raging river below. He turned in shock, only to hear another plank snap... and then another. The bridge was collapsing one step at a time. Panic filled his mind. Running could make the bridge collapse even faster, but standing still meant the next plank beneath his feet could give way at any second. Taking a deep breath, he carefully placed one foot forward, moving slowly as the river roared beneath him and the bridge continued breaking apart behind his back. Only a few unstable planks remained between him and safety. Gathering every ounce of courage, he leaped toward solid ground just as the last section of the bridge gave way. He landed safely, and moments later the entire bridge crashed into the river with a deafening roar. At sunrise, the villagers arrived to find the bridge completely destroyed. As they searched the muddy ground for clues, they noticed something impossible. There was only one trail of footprints... and they ended halfway across the bridge. Beyond that point, there were no footprints leading to safety. To this day, no one has ever been able to explain how the traveler escaped.
