Two hikers entered a dense forest just before sunset, planning to be back within an hour.But they took the wrong trail. As darkness covered the trees, they realized they were completely lost. Their phones had no signal, and every direction looked the same. Then they heard footsteps behind them. At first, they assumed it was another lost hiker. But every time they stopped walking the footsteps stopped too. Terrified, they ran until they found an old abandoned cabin hidden deep in the woods. They locked the door and waited in complete silence. Then came three slow knocks. Knock Knock Knock Neither of them dared to move Hours later, when the sun finally rose, they opened the cabin door No one was there But in the mud outside, there was only one set of footprints They began at the cabin door and disappeared into the forest There were no footprints leading toward the cabin To this day, no one can explain who or what knocked that night.
